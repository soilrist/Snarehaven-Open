## Description (≤4000)
```
Hunt animals on an island of forests, snowfields and open sea, wear them down and capture them, then leave the captured animals on your ranch. Even while you are away, your ranch keeps collecting items as time passes.

HUNT AND CAPTURE
- The tool in your hand is your weapon: axe, pickaxe, fishing rod, even a rifle.
- Defeat an animal for materials, or weaken it and throw a lasso to capture it.
- Every captured animal is recorded in your codex.

IDLE RANCH
- Put captured animals in your ranch slots and they produce items as time passes.
- Harvest the items to use as crafting materials or sell them at the shop.
- Decorate your ranch animals and your ranch grounds.

CO-OP FOR 4 TO 8 PLAYERS
- Gather friends in one room and explore the same world together. No competition, just cooperative PVE.
- Visit a friend's base and shake their fruit tree to bring their fruit home.

FISHING AND THE SEA
- Cast your line and tap fast to reel the fish in.
- Board a boat and sail out to the edge of the island.

CRAFT, COOK, BUILD
- Craft tools and gear from what you gather, and cook meals to restore health.
- Start with a tent and grow your base into a house.
- Mine ore and smelt it into ingots to build better tools.

SWEET POTATO MARKET
- A sweet potato merchant visits only on Sundays. Buy sweet potatoes, watch the prices rise and fall through the week, and sell them to the general store.
- Sweet potatoes rot when the next Sunday arrives, so timing is everything.

FRUIT TREES
- Every player has one native fruit. Bring home a friend's fruit and plant it on your ranch to grow a tree that bears fruit again and again.
- Saplings grow into fruiting trees, and a grown tree can be chopped down with an axe.

LASSO MACHINE
- A gacha that you play with coins earned in the game. The odds for each rarity are shown on the pull screen.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Free to play. iPhone only, played in landscape.
- An internet connection is required. Your progress is saved on a server.
- You can request account deletion inside the app.
- Contact: snarehaven@gmail.com
```
